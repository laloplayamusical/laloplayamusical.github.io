Beach Ball Fun ¡A jugar con la pelota!

Lalo the baby manatee plays catch with a friend on the beach. A sweet way to talk about sharing and taking turns.

⬇ Download free PDF

US Letter size, prints in black and white. Ages 2-5.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Say the title in Spanish together: ¡A jugar con la pelota!
  • Name the colors in both languages: azul / blue, rojo / red, amarillo / yellow.
  • Play the song while coloring, then ask your child to sing the chorus.
